QED coupling at x = t = 0

We calculate $J^\mu = \bar{u}_2 \gamma^\mu u_1$ and $M = e A_\mu J^\mu$, with $e=1$ and metric $(+---)$. This is the local bilinear corresponding to the interaction term $e A_\mu \bar{\psi} \gamma^\mu \psi$.
